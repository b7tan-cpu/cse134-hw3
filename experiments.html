MathML: mass-energy equivalence

E=mc2

Here, E is energy, m is mass, and c is the speed of light.

Being included in a specification does not guarantee consistent browser implementation. MathML was supported in Firefox and Safari earlier, while Chromium-based browsers lacked dependable enabled support for years. Chrome briefly supported it in version 24, removed it because of instability, and enabled it again in version 109. That gap made native MathML difficult to use for a broad audience.

Source: Can I use: MathML browser support (checked October 14, 2026).
